# Privacy Policy for TempoRep

_Last updated: [FILL IN DATE BEFORE PUBLISHING]_

This policy describes how TempoRep ("the app") handles information. It reflects exactly what the app's source code does — nothing more.

## Summary

TempoRep does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

## What TempoRep stores, and where

All data below is saved locally on your device using Apple's standard `UserDefaults` storage. None of it is sent anywhere — TempoRep makes no network requests at all.

- **Workout settings** (tempo, reps, sets, rest time, unilateral mode, language preference)
- **Workout history** (tempo, sets, reps, and time under tension for completed workouts)
- **Custom presets** you save
- **Recently used exercises** (to show a "Recent" list in the exercise picker)

## What TempoRep does not do

- No account or sign-in of any kind
- No network connections — the app works entirely offline, including its exercise database (bundled with the app, not fetched from a server)
- No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs
- No tracking, and no data shared with third parties, because none is collected in the first place
- No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera

## Permissions the app uses

- **Speech synthesis** (on-device, via Apple's `AVSpeechSynthesizer`) to speak workout cues. This does not send audio or text anywhere.
- **Background audio** so voice/sound cues and your music keep playing during a workout, even with the screen locked. No audio is recorded or transmitted.

## Deleting your data

Since everything is stored only on your device, uninstalling the app removes all of it. There is nothing stored elsewhere to delete.

## Children's privacy

TempoRep does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If TempoRep's data practices ever change (for example, if a future version adds an optional cloud sync feature), this policy will be updated to describe exactly what changed before that version ships.
