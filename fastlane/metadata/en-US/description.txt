TempoRep is a minimal, high-contrast tempo training timer for strength workouts.

In tempo training, every rep follows a 4-digit tempo like 4010: eccentric (lowering), pause, concentric (lifting), pause — each in seconds. TempoRep counts you through every phase with a huge, glanceable display and spoken cues, so you never have to look away from your lift to check a stopwatch.

FEATURES

- Big, high-contrast display readable from across the room, with a countdown ring for the current phase
- Spoken voice cues that count each phase in real time ("Down, 2, 3, 4"), plus distinct sounds and haptics for every phase, rep, set, and side change
- Unilateral (single-side) mode: do all reps on one side, then the other, with a configurable switch pause
- A bundled database of 873 exercises with search and filters — pick one and TempoRep suggests sensible defaults, like starting with the lift for deadlifts and pull-ups
- Save your own presets, or use the built-in ones, to jump straight into a workout
- Full history of every workout: tempo, sets, reps, and time under tension
- Background audio: your music keeps playing, and cues keep coming even with the screen locked
- Available in English, Dutch, French, and German, following your device's language automatically

TempoRep works entirely on your device. No account, no network connection, no tracking — your workout data never leaves your phone.
